# Marketplace And Revenue Memory

## Product Outcome
Demand reaches qualified local providers quickly; the requester chooses the provider; providers retain service pricing and contracts.

## Required Flow
- Service-area/specialty discovery and provider priority rules.
- Anonymized request before provider confirmation.
- Availability response, requester selection, contact release, completion, and rating.
- Provider standing and marketplace availability controls.
- Reliable subscriptions, Connect, payouts, platform fees, webhooks, refunds, and idempotency where applicable.

## Boundaries
- Do not conflate provider capture billing, presentation billing, or platform fees.
- Do not release private requester details before the approved confirmation point.
- Frontiers|3D does not silently set provider service prices.

## Current Gap
Complete launch acceptance across matching, notifications, work orders, ratings, payments, payouts, and refunds; keep public handbook rules synchronized with implementation.
